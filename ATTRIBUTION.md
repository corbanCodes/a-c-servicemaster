## Removed 25 Sep 2026

Every U.S. Air Force photograph and the CC BY 2.0 photo from another HVAC company (Vernon Air
Conditioning) has been **taken off the site**. The Air Force shots were genuine HVAC work but the
uniforms and desert backdrops read as a military deployment rather than as a contractor in South
Carolina, and the Vernon photo showed an identifiable technician who is not Paul. Paul's own
photos replaced all of them, which is the outcome the earlier notes were asking for.


## Logo

`assets/img/logo.png` and `assets/img/logo-light.png` are built from the logo Paul supplied in
`new-logo/`. The original is a 1774x887 transparent PNG — plenty of resolution, and its navy,
blue and orange happen to match the site palette almost exactly.

**The wordmark is navy, so the supplied file is invisible on the dark footer.** `logo-light.png`
is the same artwork with the navy recoloured to white for that one placement. If the logo is ever
replaced, both variants need regenerating.

## Video

`assets/video/our-work.mp4` is a 44-second silent reel built here from twenty of Paul's own job
photographs — a slow Ken Burns move on each, cross-cut, 1280x720, about 3.5 MB. No stock footage
and no third-party clips. If he ever shoots real video, this is the file to replace.
