Why the slogan is “we move heat”

Your Air Conditioner Does Not Make Cold Air

It moves heat. That is the entire job, it is where the name comes from, and once you know it most of what goes wrong stops being mysterious.

  1. 1
    In summer, it moves heat out.

    Refrigerant picks up heat from the air inside your house, carries it outside, and dumps it out of the outdoor unit. The cold is just what is left behind.

  2. 2
    In winter, a heat pump runs it backwards.

    The same cycle reverses. Even on a cold day there is heat in the outside air, and the system pulls it in. That is why a heat pump costs less to run than strip heat.

  3. 3
    So a breakdown is one of three things.

    The heat stopped moving because of refrigerant, because of airflow, or because of electrical. Working out which one before any parts get replaced is the whole job.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions worth asking any HVAC company in Aiken County.

What areas do you cover?
North Augusta, Aiken, and the rest of Aiken County — Belvedere, Clearwater, Graniteville, Langley, Bath, Warrenville, Burnettown, Beech Island, Jackson and New Ellenton among them. If you are near the edge of that, call and ask — the answer is usually yes.
My system is not cooling. Anything to check before I call?
Three quick things. Check that the filter is not filthy, check that the breaker for the outdoor unit has not tripped, and check that the thermostat is calling for cool and its batteries are good. One thing not to do: if you see ice on the refrigerant lines or the indoor coil, switch the system off and leave it off. Running it iced up is how a bad afternoon turns into a compressor.
Do you work on heat pumps as well as gas furnaces?
Both. Heat pumps, gas furnaces, air handlers, package units and split systems. Heat pumps are the majority of what is installed around here, and their winter faults — defrost problems, systems locked on emergency heat, reversing-valve trouble — are routine work.
Will you tell me if a repair is not worth doing?
Yes, with the reasoning. Age of the system, what the repair costs, what is likely to fail next, and what a replacement would run. You get the numbers and you make the call — there is no commission riding on the answer.
How can I pay, and is there financing?
Cash and credit cards are both fine, and financing is available on larger jobs such as a full system replacement. There is also a 10% senior discount. Ask when you call and the options get explained before any work is booked.
Do you take light commercial work?
Yes — small offices, retail spaces, shops and churches, including rooftop and package units. Larger industrial plants are outside what one technician should be promising to cover, so those get an honest referral instead.
